laptop-backup

A personal backup tool for one laptop.

laptop-backup copies encrypted backup snapshots of its owner's laptop to that owner's own Google Drive, so a copy survives if the laptop is lost, stolen or broken. It is used by its owner only and is not offered to anyone else.

What it does with Google Drive

  • It signs in with the drive.file permission: it can see and change only the files it created itself, and nothing else in the Drive.
  • Everything it uploads is encrypted on the laptop before it leaves. Google stores only ciphertext.
  • It never reads, shares or sells any other data.
