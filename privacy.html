laptop-backup privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

laptop-backup is a personal backup tool used by its owner only, on the owner's own laptop and with the owner's own Google account.

Data it accesses

Through the Google Drive API with the drive.file scope, it accesses only the files and folders it created itself in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot see any other file in that Drive.

How the data is used

It uploads, lists, reads back and removes its own encrypted backup files, solely to keep an off-site copy of the owner's laptop backups. The backup data is encrypted on the laptop before upload; the key never leaves the laptop.

Sharing

No data is shared with, sold to or disclosed to anyone. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking, and this site sets no cookies.

laptop-backup's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Backup files are kept according to the owner's backup schedule and deleted when they expire. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
